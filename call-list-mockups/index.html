Try the whole flow — the Zen mockup is fully interactive:
  • Hit Call now → 1-2s of dialing, then a random Connected / No answer outcome (hot leads pick up more).
  • Tap an outcome (Interested, Wants info, No answer, etc.) → the lead disappears from today's list, a toast confirms what happened, and the KPI counters bump.
  • Add notes in the contact panel — they appear in the timeline on the same lead.
  • Set a callback time when logging Interested and the callbacks-today card + hero updates.
  • Snooze a lead for 1h from the contact panel and it disappears until later.
