Call list · alternative mockups

Four different takes on /call-list, each designed to reduce rep overwhelm and rethink the modal-heavy calling flow. Same placeholder data across all four, so the shape of the queue is comparable. Open one to click around.

Mockup 1

Focus Mode — one contact at a time

Card-Rolodex UX: rep sees a single, spacious contact card. A "3 of 10" progress bar sets a finite goal for the day. Outcome logging is a big-target grid embedded right below the dial row — no modal at all.

1 card visible Callbacks Today card (right rail) Inline outcome grid J/K keyboard nav
Mockup 2

Zen Stack — 10 rows + slide drawer

Clean list of only 10 leads at a time with a "Load next 10" button. Big pink "Callbacks Today" hero sits above everything. Clicking any lead slides a full-height drawer in from the right with dial, outcome, callback picker, notes — one flow, no modal blocking the screen.

10 at a time Callbacks Today hero Right slide drawer Priority filter chips
Mockup 3

Today's Agenda — timeline view

Everything is a scheduled item on a vertical timeline: callbacks, cold calls, meetings, breaks. A subtle "● NOW" band highlights what to work on. Callbacks Today ribbon at the top jumps you to the slot. Click a slot to expand it inline — no modal, no drawer. Includes a friendly break-nudge card.

Time-anchored slots "NOW" auto-expand Inline expand (no modal) Break-reminder card
Mockup 4

Command Center — split view

Dark, dashboard-style layout with a KPI strip up top, a paginated left list (10 per page), and a persistent right-hand detail panel that updates instantly on selection. Modal is completely gone. Calendar tucks into a slide-out tab on the right edge so it doesn't fight for space.

Paginated 10-per-page Persistent detail panel KPI strip at top Collapsible calendar tab
Ideas shared across mockups — each of these could be lifted individually into the live page:
  • Callbacks Today card above the calendar (mockups 1, 2, 3 — hero card; mockup 4 — dedicated KPI tile).
  • Cap what's visible (10 focus / 10 stack / 10 per page / 1 at a time) — reduces the "wall of leads" feeling.
  • Kill the dial modal: mockups 1 & 3 log outcomes inline, mockup 2 uses a right drawer, mockup 4 uses a persistent side panel.
  • Big-target outcome grid with sub-labels ("book callback", "email info pack", "recycle tomorrow") — same 6-7 buttons across all four, but no scrolling required.
  • Keyboard nav (J/K) in mockups 1 & 4 — reps can rip through the list without touching the mouse.
  • Ambient calendar — always visible but visually softer (side rail in 1/2, timeline slots in 3, collapsible tab in 4).
