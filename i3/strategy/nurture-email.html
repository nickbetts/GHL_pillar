i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Email Drips

Relationship-layer email workflows for compliance-led nurturing and proof-driven conversion support.

Nurturing | Newsletters & Automated Email Drips

Relationship Layer

1. The 2026 Digital Landscape Context Hooks

Every automated follow-up sequence should tie into a current, relevant shift so the content feels timely and useful:

  • AI search shift angle: Position i3MEDIA as helping brands stay discoverable as buyers move to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI answers. Keep claims specific and evidence-led.
  • Marketing consent angle: Emphasise clean, compliant email and consent practices (PECR/GDPR) as part of a joined-up marketing setup, validating any legal interpretation with up-to-date ICO guidance before publication.

2. Low-Friction Lead Magnets

Capture contact entries through useful resources before pitching a full discovery call:

  • Resource Asset A: How to Choose a Digital Agency: The 10-Point Checklist for Getting It Right.
  • Resource Asset B: The 2026 AI Search Playbook: Getting Your Brand Found in ChatGPT and Google AI Answers.

3. Step-by-Step Value-First Email Drip Workflows

Email 1: Immediate Asset Delivery & Quick Win

  • Timing: Sent instantly upon download trigger.
  • Subject: Here is the guide you asked for
  • Body Delivery Profile: Deliver the PDF link straight away, no preamble. Add a warm practical note: pick one quick win from the guide (for example, fixing your homepage headline or page speed) and explain briefly how i3MEDIA helps teams action these without the usual back-and-forth.

Email 2: The Core Pain Point (The Patchwork Problem)

  • Timing: 3 days post Email 1.
  • Subject: Quick question about your marketing setup
  • Body Delivery Profile: Plain text, peer-to-peer tone. Start with a relatable picture of juggling a designer, an SEO and a paid-ads freelancer who never talk to each other. Do not lecture, just name it warmly and with a little knowing humour. Then move to the practical point: how much time and momentum is lost coordinating suppliers who should be working as one team? That is the thing i3MEDIA fixes.

Email 3: The Leadership Validation Layer (Proving ROI)

  • Timing: 4 days post Email 2.
  • Subject: Three things your board will ask about next quarter
  • Body Delivery Profile: Friendly but useful. Frame it as "here is something worth preparing for" rather than a warning. Walk through the three questions that come up most in leadership conversations: which channels drive enquiries, what is our cost per lead, and are we visible in AI search. Then show honestly why they are hard to answer when web, SEO and paid are handled by different suppliers. Include a simple one-page reporting layout they can actually use.

Email 4: Proof-Led Walkthrough Invitation

  • Timing: 7 days post Email 3.
  • Subject: How Eden Shopping Centre lifted retailer enquiries by 20%
  • Body Delivery Profile: Lead with the outcome, not the feature. Tell the story in plain conversational language: what they were doing before, what changed, what it feels like now. End warmly with something like "if that sounds familiar, it is worth 15 minutes to see if i3MEDIA could do the same for you" and link to the discovery-call booking page.
