i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: LinkedIn Retargeting

Validation-layer paid social for warm audience recapture and lead form completion.

Retargeting/Research | LinkedIn Retargeting Ads

Validation Layer

1. Segment Pool Parameters

Populate LinkedIn Matched Audiences through strict user-action signals:

  • Website Performance Segment: Users tracking across i3media.net over a rolling 90-day window, filtered by visits to services or case-study pages.
  • Form Abandonment Segment: High-priority targets who accessed the discovery-call booking flow but closed out before completing it.
  • Engagement Pools: Newsletter subscribers and individuals who interacted with top-of-funnel Single Image or Document items.

2. Eight Creative Concept Families

Deploy varied messaging angles with native LinkedIn formats (Text Ads, Spotlight, Single Image, Carousel, Video, Lead Gen Forms) targeting warm-intent segments at scale:

Concept 1: The Website That Went Quiet

Format: Single Image + Text Overlay

Headline: Your website just stopped bringing in enquiries
Body: "Actually, it stopped months ago. You just didn't notice."
Overlay Text: PRETTY BUT INVISIBLE
CTA: Book a 15-min discovery call

Angle: Personifies a good-looking site that no longer performs. Plays into the fear of missed enquiries. Expected CTR: 2.8-3.5%

Concept 2: The Cost of Four Suppliers

Format: Carousel (3 cards)

Card 1: "You're paying a designer, an SEO and a paid-ads freelancer separately"
Card 2: "That's three retainers, three invoices and hours of management time"
Card 3: "i3MEDIA gives you one integrated team, from strategy to execution."
Overlay Text: STOP THE SUPPLIER SPRAWL

Angle: Quantifies the hidden cost of managing multiple suppliers. Data-driven, decision-maker resonant. Expected CTR: 3.2-4.1%

Concept 3: The Disjointed Brand Map

Format: Single Image (flowchart-style)

Visual: Messy arrows bouncing between website, social, ads and email with X marks and mixed branding
Headline: Your brand looks different on every channel
Body: Website says one thing, ads another, social a third. One team joins it all up.
Overlay Text: JOIN IT ALL UP

Angle: Visual complexity overwhelm. Relatable marketing frustration. Expected CTR: 3.0-3.8%

Concept 4: The Marketing ROI Scorecard

Format: Spotlight Ad + Lead Gen Form

Headline: [First Name], can you prove your marketing ROI?
Body: Most teams can't tell which channels actually drive enquiries. Get your free growth audit.
Form CTA: I want a growth audit
Overlay Text: WHERE ARE YOUR LEADS COMING FROM?

Angle: Personalized benchmark anxiety + data magnet. High-intent audience. Expected Form Completion: 18-25%

Concept 5: The Agency Comparison Smackdown

Format: Carousel (4 cards)

Card 1: "Digital agency comparison (honest version)"
Card 2: [Freelancers] + [DIY builder] + [SEO-only agency] stacked with gaps highlighted
Card 3: "i3MEDIA" with all gaps filled + AI-search capability
Card 4: "Skip 6 months of shopping around. Talk to us."
Overlay Text: ONE TEAM ADVANTAGE

Angle: Competitive differentiation without naming competitors directly. Decision-stage messaging. Expected CTR: 2.5-3.2%

Concept 6: The Peak Season Readiness Checklist

Format: Single Image + Text (seasonal hook)

Headline: Your biggest sales season is coming. Are you ready?
Body: ☐ Website fast & conversion-ready ☐ Campaigns scheduled ☐ SEO & paid aligned ☐ Tracking in place. Stop improvising.
Overlay Text: PREP NOW FOR PEAK SEASON

Angle: Seasonal urgency (Black Friday, campaign launches, sector peaks). Universally relevant pain point. Expected CTR: 3.5-4.3%

Concept 7: The Supplier Sprawl Roast

Format: Video (15-20 sec, UGC style)

Scene: Marketer opens laptop, emails the web guy, then the SEO freelancer, then the ads person... looks at camera defeated.
Voiceover: "This is your life with four separate suppliers."
Scene 2: Same marketer, now on one call with the i3MEDIA team, smiling, closing all other tabs.
Voiceover: "This is your life with i3MEDIA."
Overlay Text: ONE TEAM. SANITY RESTORED.

Angle: Relatable comedy. Emotional/aspirational. High engagement potential. Expected Video View Rate: 45-55%

Concept 8: The Trust & Proof Stack

Format: Lead Gen Form (Retargeting Close)

Headline: See how Eden Shopping Centre lifted retailer enquiries by 20%
Body: 30 min conversation. No sales pitch. Real results. Download the case study.
Overlay Text: PROVEN RESULTS
Form Fields: First Name, Last Name, Work Email, Role, Company Size

Angle: Social proof trigger + educational resource. Lower-friction ask. Expected Form Completion: 22-30%

4. Offer and Destination Mapping

  • Form starters: send to short booking route with low-friction scheduling copy.
  • Document engagers: route to deeper educational assets before hard CTA.
  • Pricing-page visitors: route to value-comparison and confidence-building assets.
  • Competitor-page visitors: route to switching frameworks and "why one team" explainers.

5. Lead Quality Controls

Audience Hygiene

  • Exclude active opportunities and booked discovery calls daily.
  • Suppress recent form submitters from repeated lead-gen asks.
  • Separate customer advocacy audiences from prospecting pools.

Performance Hygiene

  • Review lead quality by ad format, not only CPL.
  • Downgrade high-volume/low-quality audiences quickly.
  • Protect budget for audience tiers with strongest meeting rates.

6. Optimisation Rhythm

  • Twice weekly: check delivery health, recency-window spend balance, and suppression integrity.
  • Weekly: review lead quality by audience and message family and refresh the weakest creative pair.
  • Monthly: re-tier audience windows based on conversion lag and sales feedback patterns.
