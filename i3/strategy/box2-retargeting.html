i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Retargeting and Research

Validation-layer execution covering LinkedIn retargeting, competitor interception, and Meta CAPI retargeting.

Retargeting/Research | LinkedIn Retargeting Ads

Validation Layer

1. Segment Pool Parameters

Populate LinkedIn Matched Audiences through strict user-action signals:

  • Website Performance Segment: Users tracking across i3media.net over a rolling 90-day window, filtered by visits to services or case-study pages.
  • Form Abandonment Segment: High-priority targets who accessed the discovery-call booking flow but closed out before completing it.
  • Engagement Pools: Newsletter subscribers and individuals who interacted with top-of-funnel Single Image or Document items.

2. The Full Nurture Format Engine

Retargeting/Research | Google Ads Competitor Interception

Conquesting Safeguard

Campaign architecture control: run competitor interception as a standalone campaign family (Search | Competitor) with its own budget, bidding, and negatives. Do not merge this term set into the Core Intent campaign.

1. Strategic Interception Vectors

Deploy exact match parameters to capture buyers looking to switch away from freelancers, DIY builders, or an underperforming agency:

  • The Patchwork Supplier Wave (multiple freelancers): Capture buyers stitching together separate freelancers who want one accountable team. Position i3MEDIA as one integrated team, strategy to execution. Bid on: "web design and seo agency", "one agency for web and marketing", [full service digital agency], "agency instead of freelancers".
  • The DIY Platform Layer (Wix / Squarespace / GoDaddy): Capture businesses outgrowing website builders that can't scale, rank or convert. Bid on: "Wix alternatives for business", "Squarespace vs professional web design", [outgrown Wix], "upgrade from Squarespace".
  • The Freelance Marketplace Gate (Fiverr / Upwork): Intercept buyers burned by cheap, inconsistent marketplace work who now want a proper agency partner. Bid on: "Fiverr web design alternative", "Upwork vs agency", [professional alternative to freelance web design].
  • Brand Defensive Guard: Bid directly on your own name brand terms (i3MEDIA, i3MEDIA agency) to block direct traffic poaching from competitors conquesting your pipeline keywords.

2. Specialised Comparison Landing Page Blueprint

Divert competitive queries to targeted pages structured to address real buyer friction:

  • The Patchwork Cost & Time Matrix: Showcase a side-by-side comparison. Itemise the true cost of juggling separate suppliers (multiple retainers, management time, finger-pointing, misaligned strategy) against i3MEDIA's one integrated team.
  • The Proof & Credentials Asset: Embed clear, downloadable summary sheets covering 20+ years of delivery, cross-sector case studies, Clutch and Google reviews, and the AI-era search capability most competitors don't offer.

Retargeting/Research | Paid Meta Retargeting (CAPI Engine)

High-Frequency Validation

1. Conversions API (CAPI) Architecture Backend

Enforce a modern tracking setup to protect custom remarketing pools from browser cookie drops:

  • Server-to-Server Integration: Deploy Meta Conversions API (CAPI) linked directly to the CRM/form layer, matching work email hash identifiers to secure high Event Match Quality indicators.
  • Exclusion Protocol Rules: Apply live exclusions to existing clients and booked discovery calls to protect daily ad spend.
  • UK Location Fee Surcharge Warning: Account for the mandatory 2% UK Location Surcharge fee parameter directly inside line-item asset models to neutralise balance errors.

2. Dynamic Andromeda Creative Concepts

Meta’s delivery engine prioritises ad variety and visual targeting over manual setting configurations. Deploy three distinct creative tracks:

Concept 1: The Supplier Sprawl Breakdown Graphic

  • Visual Style: High-contrast graphic map titled "The True Price of Four Suppliers."
  • Ad Angle: Visually chart the duplicated cost and management time of paying separate freelancers and agencies, positioning i3MEDIA as one integrated, cost-effective team.

Concept 2: The Website Rebuild Screen Capture Video

  • Visual Style: A clean, user-generated-content (UGC) styled 20-second vertical screen flow.
  • Ad Angle: Show a slow, dated site transformed into a fast, mobile-first, conversion-ready build with clear calls to action and enquiries landing.

Concept 3: The AI Search Advantage

  • Visual Style: Clean carousel step-by-step display highlighting search visibility gains.
  • Ad Angle: Contrast a brand invisible in ChatGPT and Google AI answers with one optimised for answer-engine and generative search by i3MEDIA.
