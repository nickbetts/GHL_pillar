i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: LinkedIn Organic

Company page and founder-led organic content for sustained authority and inbound trust.

1st Touch & Nurture Touch Layers | LinkedIn Organic Suite (Company & Personal Pages)

Brand & Personal Authority

1. The Execution Strategy Balance Matrix

Organic execution is distributed across a clear content mix: Static Technical Updates (48%), Video or Reels Walkthroughs (22%), Educational Carousels (18%), and Temporary Interactive Stories (12%).

  • The Corporate Anchor Page: Showcases recent work, client results, service explainers, and the AI-era search perspective that sets i3MEDIA apart.
  • The Founder's Personal Profile Page: Designed to serve as the dominant pipeline generation driver, humanising the brand through direct storytelling and unvarnished takes on web, marketing, and AI-search trends.

2. Founder's Personal Page Core Theme Blueprints

Theme A: The "Pretty But Invisible" Website Teardown

Hook Expression: A client came to us last month with a beautiful website that Google had never heard of. Design without discoverability is just expensive decoration.

Body Narrative Structure: Walk through the real cost of a good-looking site that doesn't convert — wasted ad spend, missed enquiries, and a brand that never gets found in search or AI answers. Keep the tone dry and knowing rather than preachy. The point lands better when it feels like something they already suspected.

Theme B: The AI Search Shift (AEO/GEO)

Hook Expression: Your customers are starting to ask ChatGPT and Google's AI what to buy. Here is what that means for your marketing in 2026.

Body Narrative Structure: Translate the shift to AI-era search into practical actions. Focus on how to stay discoverable when answers, not links, become the front page. Include a note to keep claims specific and evidence-led.

3. Personal Profile Optimisation Framework

Systematically structure profile pathways to turn casual profile views into qualified bookable leads:

  • Banner Strategy: High-impact visual display reading: "Web, SEO, Paid & Brand under one roof. One team, from strategy to execution." Contains an arrow guiding focus directly to the target header URL block.
  • The Featured Asset Pipeline: Pin three explicit conversion gates to your profile:
    1. High-utility guide download link: "How to Choose a Digital Agency."
    2. Tangible proof case study (e.g. Eden Shopping Centre — 20% uplift in retailer enquiries).
    3. Direct smart schedule link routing back to /book-a-discovery-call parameters.
