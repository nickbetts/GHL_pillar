i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Google Ads

High-intent BOFU capture for web design, SEO, PPC, eCommerce and full-service agency searches across sectors.

1st Touch | Google Ads (High Intent BOFU Terms)

Paid Interception

1. Core Targeting and Geo-Location Parameters

Traffic capture is bounded strictly by geography and transactional intent rules to shield the budget from global click variance.

  • Bidding Implementation: Maximise Conversions (Target CPA constraint applied post initial verification pass).
  • Geo-Targeting Restrictions: UK-wide, weighted toward commercial demand centres: London, Greater Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Bristol, Peterborough & East of England, Glasgow, Edinburgh, and Cardiff.

4.3 Asset Testing Priorities (First 30 Days)

  • Headlines: test one pain-led line, one solution-led line, and one trust-led line per ad group in every RSA.
  • Descriptions: pair one outcome-led description with one credibility-led description.
  • Sitelinks: rotate Book a Discovery Call and Our Work into all ad groups and test service-specific links by intent.
  • Callouts: keep 6-8 live at all times and swap lowest-performing assets fortnightly.
  • Quality controls: pin only where compliance requires it and otherwise preserve machine-learning flexibility for RSA combinations.
