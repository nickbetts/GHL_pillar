i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Execution Chapter 2 (Technical SEO and LLM)

Joint dev and marketing implementation requirements for schema, metadata, authoring governance, and LLM files.

Execution Chapter 2: Technical SEO, Schema, Authoring & LLM Discovery

Marketing + Dev Joint Plan

1. Required Schema Entity Stack

Schema Type Required Properties Applies To
Organization name, url, logo, sameAs, contactPoint, areaServed, description Homepage, agency-level pages
WebSite SearchAction Sitewide template
Service / Offer name, description, provider, serviceType, areaServed, offers Service pages (Web, SEO, Paid, Brand, eCommerce)
FAQPage Question and Answer text must match visible page copy exactly Service pages, discovery-call page
Article + Person + BreadcrumbList headline, author, datePublished, dateModified, mainEntityOfPage, author profile fields All guides/blog content

2. Page Mapping Minimum

  • Homepage: Organization, WebSite, Service overview.
  • Service pages: Service + Offer + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList.
  • Sector/case-study pages: Service/CreativeWork + Review + BreadcrumbList.
  • Book a discovery call page: Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList.
  • Article pages: Article + Person + BreadcrumbList + editorial metadata.

3. Authoring and Metadata Governance

  • Every indexable article must have a named author linked to an author profile page.
  • All author pages must include Person entity fields and expertise scope.
  • All articles must show publish and modified dates visibly and in metadata.
  • Minimum head tags: canonical, OG title/description/image, X card equivalents.

4. LLM Discovery Files

Root files required: /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, maintained monthly with owner, version, and last-updated fields.
  • llms.txt: Canonical high-value URLs and usage statement.
  • llms-full.txt: Machine-readable summaries of core service pages, FAQs, and guides.
  • Governance: Align robots, sitemap, and canonical policies with LLM file URL inventory.

5. AI Search Optimisation (AIO / AEO / GEO)

Buyers increasingly start in ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews. This is our fourth discovery pillar alongside SEO, and every client build should be optimised to be the answer, not just a link.

Discipline What It Covers Implementation Requirements
AIO (AI Optimisation) Being cited and summarised accurately inside AI-generated answers. Clear entity definitions, concise answer-first paragraphs, factual consistency across pages, and up-to-date llms files.
AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) Winning featured answers and AI Overviews for high-intent questions. Question-led H2/H3 structure, exact-match FAQPage schema, 40-60 word direct answers, and supporting evidence blocks.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) Influencing how generative engines represent the brand and sector expertise. Strong Organization/Person entities, third-party citations (Clutch, Google reviews, press), and consistent sameAs signals.
  • Answer-first authoring: Lead each key page and article with a direct, quotable answer before supporting detail.
  • Entity clarity: Define who i3MEDIA is, what we do, and who we serve in machine-readable, consistent language across the site.
  • Citation building: Grow the external references AI engines trust (Clutch profile, Google Business Profile reviews, awards, press mentions).
  • Monitoring: Track brand and service mentions in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews monthly, and log accuracy corrections needed.
