i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Execution Chapter 3 (Trust and Reviews Sprint)

Trust-programme execution and six-week Clutch, Google, and DesignRush review sprint with milestone targets, KPI rules, and governance checks.

Execution Chapter 3: Trust Building & Agency Reviews 6-Week Sprint

Trust Acceleration Workstream

1. Trust Architecture (Multi-Layer)

  • Layer A: Third-party validation across the agency directories buyers actually check. Clutch is the primary choice for verified B2B agency reviews and buyer credibility. Google Business Profile reviews carry the most weight for local and brand search. DesignRush and Trustpilot add further trust signals with low overhead.
  • Layer B: Onsite trust modules (client logos, attributed testimonials, quantified results, risk-reversal blocks).
  • Layer C: Paid trust creatives (testimonial cards, results proof such as Eden Shopping Centre's 20% enquiry uplift, onboarding confidence).
  • Layer D: Sales proof pack (objection handling for switching agency, cost, timelines, ownership, and results).

2. Awards to Target

Use awards to turn proof into external validation. The table below covers agency awards we can enter directly, plus sector awards where a joint entry with a client and a clear results story is the strongest route. Confirm current entry fees and deadlines on each awarding body's site before committing.

Award Who enters Best categories for us Notes Why it fits
UK Agency Awards Us as a full-service agency Best Full-Service Agency, Best Website, Best SEO Campaign, Best Use of Paid Media Annual cycle. Check ukagencyawards.com for current categories, fees, and deadlines. Directly recognises full-service and channel-specific agency work. A shortlist or win is a strong buyer-facing trust signal.
The Drum Awards (Digital / Design / Search) Us, or jointly with a client Website of the Year, Best SEO/Content, Best Use of Paid, plus sector-specific categories Multiple award programmes run through the year. Check thedrum.com/awards for the right cycle. The Drum is a leading marketing industry title. Recognition here reaches marketing decision-makers well beyond any single sector.
Wirehive / regional agency awards Us as an agency Best Large Agency, Best Website, Best eCommerce, Best SEO Regional and national agency award programmes. Confirm eligibility and fees per programme. Strong peer and buyer credibility, and useful for regional visibility around Peterborough and the East of England.
UK eCommerce Awards Jointly with an eCommerce client Best eCommerce Website, Best Use of Search, Best Small/Medium Retailer Site Annual. Enter with a strong retail/eCommerce client and measurable results. Plays directly to our largest portfolio sector. A joint entry with a retail client and clear revenue uplift is a compelling story.
Clutch Leaders / Global Awards Us, driven by verified reviews Top B2B / Web Design / SEO / Digital Marketing agency in the UK Earned through verified Clutch reviews and profile strength rather than a paid entry. Buyers actively use Clutch to shortlist agencies. Leader placements convert directly into enquiries.
Charity Times / Third Sector Awards Jointly with a charity client Digital Innovation, Best Website, Marketing Campaign of the Year, with our work named Sector awards. Enter alongside a charity client with a clear impact story. Charity is one of our proof pillars. A joint win reinforces credibility in a sector where trust matters, without positioning us as charity-only.

3. Golden Case Studies (Minimum 3)

We need at least 3 golden case studies before awards entries, trust assets, and sales proof are finalised. Aim for a spread across sectors, not just one.

Golden Case Study 1 (Retail)

Theme: Website and search driving measurable enquiries.

Should prove: Eden Shopping Centre's 20% boost in retailer online enquiries, with a clear before/after and named testimonial.

Golden Case Study 2 (eCommerce)

Theme: Full rebuild and growth over time.

Should prove: revenue growth (for example GreenGate 5x in 18 months), stronger conversion, and a site that scales with the business.

Golden Case Study 3 (Charity or Healthcare)

Theme: Campaign performance and clarity of results.

Should prove: better attribution, stronger enquiry or donation growth, and clear reporting leadership can act on.

4. Review Milestone Targets

Agency trust is built on verified reviews across the platforms buyers check. Use these as internal milestones to drive review-collection effort with happy clients.

Milestone Target criteria How to use it
Clutch verified reviews Minimum 10 verified client reviews with strong overall rating Homepage trust block, paid social creative, sales deck, and Clutch Leader eligibility
Google Business Profile reviews Steady flow of 4.5+ star reviews with response management Boosts local and brand search visibility; embed rating on key landing pages
Clutch Leader placement High review strength and profile completeness for the target category and region Flagship trust asset. Use across all channels once earned
DesignRush profile Complete profile with portfolio and client reviews Embed badge on the discovery-call landing page and in email nurture
Trustpilot Verified review volume with strong TrustScore Additional consumer-facing trust signal for the onsite trust block
