i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Google Competitor Interception

Standalone search campaign family to intercept active competitor-switching intent.

Retargeting/Research | Google Ads Competitor Interception

Conquesting Safeguard

Campaign architecture control: run competitor interception as a standalone campaign family (Search | Competitor) with its own budget, bidding, and negatives. Do not merge this term set into the Core Intent campaign.

1. Strategic Interception Vectors

Deploy exact match parameters to capture buyers actively looking to switch away from freelancers, DIY builders, or an underperforming agency:

  • The Patchwork Supplier Wave (multiple freelancers): Capture buyers stitching together a designer, an SEO and a paid-ads freelancer who want one accountable team. Position i3MEDIA as one integrated team, strategy to execution. Bid on: "web design and seo agency", "one agency for web and marketing", [full service digital agency], "agency instead of freelancers".
  • The Big-Agency Alternative Layer: Position i3MEDIA as the senior, responsive alternative to large agencies with high overheads and slow turnaround. Bid on: "digital agency alternative", "more responsive digital agency", "mid size digital agency UK", "agency for growing businesses".
  • The DIY Platform Layer (Wix / Squarespace / GoDaddy): Capture businesses outgrowing website builders that can't scale, rank or convert. Bid on: "Wix alternatives for business", "Squarespace vs professional web design", "outgrown Wix", "upgrade from Squarespace".
  • The Freelance Marketplace Gate (Fiverr / Upwork / PeoplePerHour): Intercept buyers burned by cheap, inconsistent marketplace work who now want a proper agency partner. Bid on: "Fiverr web design alternative", "Upwork vs agency", "professional alternative to freelance web design".
  • The Underperforming-Agency Switch Layer: Capture teams whose current agency isn't delivering results or communication. Bid on: "switch digital agency", "change web design agency", "new SEO agency", "agency not delivering results".
  • The AI-Search Readiness Layer: Capture forward-looking brands whose current provider offers no answer-engine or generative-search strategy. Bid on: "answer engine optimisation agency", "generative engine optimisation UK", "AI search agency", "get found in ChatGPT search".
  • Brand Defensive Guard: Bid directly on your own name brand terms (i3MEDIA, i3MEDIA agency, i3media reviews) to block direct traffic poaching from competitors conquesting your pipeline keywords.

1.1 Competitor Universe (Current)

Maintain this as the active interception set and refresh quarterly. Track DIY platforms, freelance marketplaces, and named regional agencies you regularly compete against:

  • wix.com (DIY website builder)
  • squarespace.com (DIY website builder)
  • godaddy.com (DIY website builder / hosting)
  • fiverr.com (freelance marketplace)
  • upwork.com (freelance marketplace)
  • peopleperhour.com (freelance marketplace)
  • Named regional / sector web & marketing agencies (populate from live pitch-loss data)
  • Large national agencies where turnaround and cost are common objections
  • SEO-only and PPC-only specialists lacking full-service and AI-search capability

2. Specialised Comparison Landing Page Blueprint

Divert competitive queries to targeted pages structured to address real buyer friction:

  • The Patchwork Cost & Time Matrix: Showcase a side-by-side comparison. Itemise the true cost of juggling separate suppliers (multiple retainers, management time, finger-pointing, misaligned strategy) against i3MEDIA's one integrated team.
  • The Proof & Credentials Asset: Embed clear, downloadable summary sheets covering 20+ years of delivery, cross-sector case studies (Eden Shopping Centre, Bombay Deli, Fullpip, Rutherford), Clutch and Google reviews, and the AI-era search capability most competitors don't offer.
