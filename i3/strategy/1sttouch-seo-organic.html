1. Target Intent Split & Persona Alignment

Content is balanced across the decision-makers who buy agency services, mapped to i3MEDIA's cross-sector market:

  • Marketing Managers & Heads of Marketing: Need a website and campaigns that actually generate enquiries, not just look good.
  • Founders & Managing Directors (SME): Want one accountable partner for design, build and marketing — no juggling multiple agencies.
  • eCommerce & Retail Owners: Focused on online sales, conversion rate, and a store that scales across locations and channels.
  • Charity Fundraising & Digital Leads: Want more supporters and donations from search, email and paid, with clean reporting.
  • Healthcare & Education Marketing Leads: Need credible, discoverable websites that drive patient/student enquiries and trust.
  • In-house teams outgrowing a freelancer or patchwork of agencies: Ready for a full-service partner that owns the outcome end to end.

Tier 3.1 Implementation Rules for Every Post

  • Title tags: keep within 55-60 characters where possible, with primary keyword in the first half.
  • Meta descriptions: target 140-155 characters with a clear value statement and soft CTA.
  • Secondary keyword placement: include at least 3 secondary phrases across H2/H3 headings and body copy without stuffing.
  • FAQ eligibility rule: only deploy FAQPage schema where the same questions and answers are fully visible on the page.
  • Internal links per post: minimum of 3 links to related guides plus 1 commercial link to the core i3media service page or discovery call route.

3. Off-Page Authority Backlink Outreach Framework

Build topical authority through a repeatable outreach system across marketing, business and sector-specific editorial ecosystems — not just one vertical.

3.1 Priority Domain Tiers (Research-Led Targeting)

  • Tier 1: Marketing & Business Media - target expert contributions, data commentary, and analysis placements on marketing, eCommerce and SME business titles (e.g. The Drum, Econsultancy, Retail Week, Business Leader, Startups.co.uk) plus relevant regional business press.
  • Tier 2: Sector & Trade Bodies - pursue webinar speaking, practical guides, and resource citations across the sectors we serve: retail & eCommerce, healthcare, education, and charity (e.g. Third Sector / Charity Digital for the charity pillar, sector associations for the others).
  • Tier 3: Directories & Peer Networks - secure high-relevance links and reviews via agency directories and B2B discovery platforms (Clutch, DesignRush, The Manifest) and local business networks.

3.2 Offer-Led Link Assets (What You Pitch)

  • Original benchmark study: annual "state of digital growth for UK SMEs" report with downloadable methodology notes.
  • Operational toolkits: website launch checklists, conversion-audit templates, and marketing ROI calculators that editors can reference.
  • Expert explainers: practical pieces on AI search (AEO/GEO), technical SEO, and paid media strategy.
  • Event speaker packs: conference session abstracts and workshop outlines mapped to live marketing and sector event themes.

3.3 Weekly Outreach Cadence (Execution Rhythm)

  • Monday: shortlist 15 targets (5 Tier 1, 5 Tier 2, 5 Tier 3), confirm editor/event owner, and align angle to current topic cycle.
  • Tuesday: send first-wave pitches with one clear hook, one proof point, and one concrete asset URL.
  • Thursday: send follow-up with fresh value add (new stat, framework visual, or quote option), not a generic reminder.
  • Friday: log outcomes, update relationship status, and move accepted opportunities into a 30-day content delivery queue.

3.4 Pitch Angles That Convert

  • AI search angle: how AEO/GEO is changing organic visibility and what brands must do now to stay discoverable.
  • Efficiency angle: the hidden cost of juggling multiple agencies vs one full-service partner that owns the outcome.
  • Performance angle: how better websites and cleaner tracking improve enquiries, conversion and marketing ROI.

3.5 Link Quality Rules and KPI Framework

  • Quality rules: prioritise context-rich editorial links, relevant anchor text, and pages that already rank for marketing and digital-services intent.
  • Monthly KPI baseline: 8-12 new referring domains, 3-5 Tier 1 placements, and at least 2 links to each core i3media asset.
  • Pipeline metrics: outreach-to-reply rate target 20%+, reply-to-placement rate target 25%+, median placement cycle under 21 days.
  • Impact metrics: growth in non-brand impressions for agency and service terms, assisted conversions from referral traffic, and uplift in discovery calls booked from linked assets.

3.6 Broken-Link Reclamation Protocol

  • Discovery: audit historical marketing, web design and SEO guides for dead outbound resources.
  • Replacement mapping: pair each dead reference with a like-for-like i3MEDIA resource (same intent, fresher data).
  • Outreach message: lead with reader value and content hygiene, then include the exact replacement URL and why it is updated.
