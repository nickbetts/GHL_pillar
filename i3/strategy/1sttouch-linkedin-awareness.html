i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: LinkedIn Ads

Targeted paid social awareness for marketing and business decision-makers across our core sectors.

1st Touch | LinkedIn Ads (Highly Targeted ICP Brand Awareness)

B2B Paid Awareness

1. Targeting Demographics & Operators Filters

Isolate account access profiles by using strict boolean filtering to reach the people who buy agency services across our sectors:

  • Targeted Company Industries: Retail & Consumer Goods, eCommerce, Hospitality & Food, Healthcare, Education, Manufacturing & Industrial, and Non-profit.
  • Account Size Tiers: Segmented into 11-200 employees (SME core) and 201-1000 employees (mid-market).
  • Job Titles & Functional Seniority: Founder, Co-Founder, Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Owner, Marketing Director, Head of Marketing, Marketing Manager, eCommerce Manager, Head of Digital, Brand Manager, Commercial Director, Director, VP, C-Level.

2. Creative Concept and Copy Hook Layouts

Marketing Manager Setup (Lead Gen & Awareness)

Format: Single Image Creative

Visual Concept: Clear split-screen showing a fragmented mix of freelancers and agencies vs one joined-up i3MEDIA team.

Copy Script: Juggling a designer, an SEO contractor and a paid-ads freelancer? Bring design, build and marketing under one roof and get one team that owns the whole outcome — strategy to execution.
Headline Options: i3MEDIA: one integrated team, from strategy to execution. / Design, build and marketing under one roof.
CTA: Learn More.

Marketing Director Setup (Thought Leadership)

Format: Document Lead Ad

Visual Concept: Dynamic multipage PDF previewing an AI-era search and growth playbook.

Copy Script: Prove marketing ROI to leadership with clean reporting on enquiries, pipeline and revenue. See how a full-service team combines web, SEO, AI search (AEO/GEO) and paid media to drive measurable growth.
Headline Options: Turn marketing activity into board-ready evidence. / The practical playbook for measurable growth.
CTA: Download.

3. Campaign Architecture and Budget Split (Best-Practice Baseline)

Campaign Layer Objective Audience Build Budget Guidance Primary Conversion Signal
Awareness Layer Reach and qualified traffic Core titles plus adjacent functions, segmented by sector and company size 40% of spend Qualified profile visits and page engagement depth
Education Layer Content consumption and problem framing Document ad and video engagers, plus site visitors with time-on-page thresholds 35% of spend High-intent content actions and repeat visits
Lead Capture Layer Lead form completions and qualified hand-raise intent Warm audiences only, with customer and booked-call suppression 25% of spend MQL-quality lead submissions

4. Creative System by Buying Stage

Awareness Creative

  • Formats: single image plus short captioned video.
  • Message style: problem-first with one clear operational pain point per asset.
  • Frequency principle: rotate hooks fortnightly to avoid audience fatigue.

Education Creative

  • Formats: document ads and carousel explainers.
  • Message style: practical frameworks, benchmark snapshots, and role-specific proof points.
  • Destination principle: route to matching educational pages, not the contact page by default.

Lead Capture Creative

  • Formats: lead gen forms and high-trust single image variants.
  • Message style: implementation clarity, expected outcomes, and next-step confidence.
  • CTA principle: use one primary CTA per ad set to preserve signal quality.

5. Lead Form and Follow-Up Handoff Standards

  • Field design: collect only essential routing fields at first touch: work email, role, organisation type, primary challenge.
  • Hidden fields: capture campaign, ad set, creative ID, and audience tier for pipeline attribution.
  • Handoff SLA: first human follow-up inside one business day for high-intent form fills.
  • Suppression rules: booked-call and existing-client status must sync back into LinkedIn audience exclusions daily.

6. Measurement and Optimisation Cadence

Cadence Primary Review Focus Action Type
Twice weekly Spend pacing, delivery anomalies, audience saturation Budget rebalance within active campaigns
Weekly Lead quality by audience and creative message Pause weak variants, promote top-performing hooks
Fortnightly Creative fatigue and message overlap Inject net-new concepts and refresh underperforming angles
Monthly Pipeline contribution and conversion quality by segment Reallocate investment to highest-yield audience clusters
