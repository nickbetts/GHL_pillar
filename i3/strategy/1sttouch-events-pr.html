i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Events & PR

Offline interception and media placement for the 1st-touch branch.

1st Touch | Events & PR Strategy

Physical Interception

1. The 2026 UK In-Person Event Target Map

Deploy operational resources to locations where marketing leaders and business owners gather to evaluate their digital growth partners — spread across our core sectors, not one vertical:

  • B2B Marketing Expo / Marketing Week events: Broad marketing decision-maker audience actively evaluating agencies and channels. b2bmarketingexpo.co.uk
  • eCommerce Expo & Retail Technology Show: Retail and online-store owners looking for web, conversion and SEO partners. ecommerceexpo.co.uk
  • Regional business & chamber events (East of England / Peterborough): Local SME pipeline near HQ, strong for relationship-led referrals.
  • Sector conferences (healthcare & education): Targeted presence at private-healthcare and education-marketing events for credibility in those sectors.
  • Charity sector events (CIOF, CharityComms, Third Sector): Maintain a presence in the charity ecosystem as one proof pillar. ciof.org.uk/learning-and-events-listing
  • Industry awards (Clutch, UK Digital Growth Awards, sector awards): Visibility opportunities for proof, partnerships, and speaking submissions.

1.1 Event Prioritisation Notes

  • Marketing/eCommerce route: Prioritise B2B Marketing Expo, eCommerce Expo and Retail Technology Show for the broadest agency-buyer pipeline.
  • Regional route: Prioritise East of England chamber and business-network events for warm, relationship-led SME leads near HQ.
  • Sector route: Use targeted charity, healthcare and education events to deepen credibility where we have proof.
  • Awards route: Use digital, agency and sector award programmes as authority signals in retargeting and nurture assets.

2. On-Site "Digital Growth Audit Station" Model

Replace static sales banners with practical, diagnostic engagement formats:

"Do not pitch service lists. Set up a physical evaluation space where a marketing lead can see a live snapshot of their website's health — search visibility, AI-answer presence, page speed, and conversion blockers. Deliver a 10-minute visual audit that highlights the enquiries they are quietly losing."

3. PR Core Placement Angles

  • Editorial Hooks: Pitch data-focused features to marketing and business outlets — original research on how AI search is changing discovery, or on the enquiries lost to slow, poorly-optimised websites.
  • Target Media Environments: Build a regular content footprint across marketing and sector media such as The Drum, Econsultancy, Retail Week, regional business press, and sector titles including Charity Digital for the charity pillar.

Use these direct links for outreach, contributor discussions, and sponsored research placements:
