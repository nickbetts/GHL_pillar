i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Inbound + Outbound Email

Relationship-layer email orchestration across inbound lead follow-up, outbound segmentation, and customer review advocacy.

Nurturing | Inbound Lead Handoff Sequence

Inbound Conversion Path

1. Trigger and Promise

Every inbound form submission should receive immediate acknowledgement and a clear next action from the team.

  • Trigger: contact form or discovery-call form submission.
  • Email 1 (instant): .
  • Tone: short, practical, human. No long sales pitch in first response.

2. Call Attempt and No-Answer Handoff

The first phone attempt should happen fast, then move to nurture only when no live conversation is reached.

  • Call SLA: first attempt within 1 business day.
  • No-answer marker: if unreachable after attempt window, mark as No Answer - Start Nurture in CRM process state.
  • Suppression rule: if call connects and a discovery call is booked, suppress nurture sequence immediately.

3. No-Answer Nurture to Discovery Call (4-Email Cadence)

Step Send Window Message Goal Primary CTA
2 days after no-answer marker Reconfirm the initial ask and reduce friction for booking. Book a 15-minute discovery call
+3 days Show one clear win (more enquiries, one team instead of four suppliers). See a relevant 90-second walkthrough
+4 days Address leadership reporting confidence and marketing ROI proof. Get the marketing reporting template
+7 days Offer direct booking with clear availability and low commitment. Pick a time this week
Suppression rule (required): if an inbound lead is already in outbound nurture, suppress outbound for 30 days (or until the discovery-call outcome is set) to avoid overlapping sequences.

Nurturing | Outbound Sequence Matrix (Type x Size)

Outbound Segmentation Path

1. Segment Framework

Outbound flows should change by both sector and organisation size so proof and pain points are relevant.

  • Sectors: Retail & eCommerce, Charity & Non-profit, Healthcare, Education, Professional Services & B2B.
  • Size bands: Small (<£1m turnover), Medium (£1m-£10m), Large (>£10m turnover).
  • Cadence baseline: 5 touches over 18 days (Day 1, 4, 8, 13, 18).

2. Sequence Blueprint by Size

Size Pain Emphasis Proof Asset CTA
Small Website not generating enquiries; stretched marketing capacity. Case snippet on enquiries or revenue uplift. Quick fit-check call
Medium Fragmented suppliers and inconsistent marketing reporting. Before/after campaign performance example. 15-minute discovery call with reporting lens
Large Multiple agencies, brand inconsistency, and proving marketing ROI. Integrated strategy and reporting briefing note. Stakeholder-led discovery session

3. Messaging Angle by Sector

Retail & eCommerce

Prioritise faster, better-converting stores, joined-up SEO and paid, and readiness for peak trading seasons.

Charity & Non-profit

Focus on supporter-friendly websites, clear campaign reporting, and getting found in search and AI answers.

Healthcare

Emphasise trustworthy, accessible websites, compliant marketing, and clear patient-enquiry journeys.

Education

Lead with clear prospectus and enrolment journeys, consistent brand across channels, and measurable enquiries.

Professional Services & B2B

Frame around credibility-building websites, lead generation, and consolidating fragmented suppliers into one team.

Nurturing | Customer Advocacy and Review Sequence

Post-Sale Trust Expansion

1. Start Point: First Measurable Win

Review asks start when the customer has seen a concrete outcome, not on arbitrary onboarding day counts.

  • Valid win triggers: first campaign completed, new website launched, or a measurable uplift in enquiries or revenue confirmed.
  • Owner: account manager sends from a named person, not a generic mailbox.
  • Policy: request honest feedback only; no incentives tied to positive reviews.

2. Four-Touch Review Ask Cadence

Touch Timing Purpose Destination
Within 24h of win Thank them, reflect their win, ask for a short review. Google Business Profile (primary)
+5 days Gentle nudge with one-click option and draft prompts. Clutch.co
+7 days Offer alternate review destinations for convenience. Trustpilot / DesignRush
+10 days If no public review, request a private testimonial quote. Internal testimonial capture form

3. Ops Guardrails and Success Metrics

  • Suppression: stop sequence once any platform review is completed.
  • Routing: if sentiment is negative, route to support recovery workflow first.
  • Core metrics: review completion rate by platform, median days-to-review after win, advocacy reply rate, and testimonial conversion rate.
Compliance note: every review ask should state that reviews are public and link to the platform privacy policy. Include clear opt-out language in all touches.
