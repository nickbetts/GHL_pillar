i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Paid Meta Retargeting

CAPI-backed warm-audience recapture with high-variation creative deployment.

Retargeting/Research | Paid Meta Retargeting (CAPI Engine)

High-Frequency Validation

1. Conversions API (CAPI) Architecture Backend

Enforce a modern tracking setup to protect custom remarketing pools from browser cookie drops:

  • Server-to-Server Integration: Deploy Meta Conversions API (CAPI) linked directly to the CRM/form layer, matching work email hash identifiers to secure high Event Match Quality indicators.
  • Exclusion Protocol Rules: Apply live exclusions to existing clients and booked discovery calls to protect daily ad spend.
  • UK Location Fee Surcharge Warning: Account for the mandatory 2% UK Location Surcharge fee parameter directly inside line-item asset models to neutralise balance errors.

2. Six Dynamic Creative Concepts (Andromeda Framework)

Meta's delivery engine prioritises ad variety and visual targeting. Deploy six distinct creative tracks with video-first emphasis:

Concept 1: The Supplier Sprawl Cost Breakdown

Format: Static Image + Carousel

Headline: Four suppliers are costing you £000s
Body: Web freelancer + SEO retainer + PPC freelancer + design contractor = duplicated cost & no joined-up strategy
Overlay: ONE TEAM. ONE INVOICE.

Expected CTR: 2.1-2.8%

Concept 2: The Website Speed Demo

Format: Vertical Video (25-30 sec)

Slow, clunky site → rebuilt fast, mobile-first, conversion-ready → enquiries start landing within days
Overlay: FAST SITES CONVERT

Expected View: 52-62% | CTR: 3.2-4.1%

Concept 3: The AI Search Win

Format: Carousel (Before/After)

Invisible in ChatGPT & Google AI → i3MEDIA: optimised for answer engines, now cited in AI results
Overlay: GET FOUND IN AI SEARCH

Expected CTR: 2.8-3.6%

Concept 4: The Customer Experience Split

Format: Vertical Video (25-35 sec)

Left: slow site, confusing checkout, dead end → Right: fast, 2-click + Apple Pay, instant confirm
Overlay: YOUR CUSTOMERS WANT THIS

Expected Completion: 48-58% | CTR: 3.5-4.3%

Concept 5: The Growth Audit Magnet

Format: Carousel + Lead Gen

Most brands can't say which channels drive enquiries. Get your benchmark: traffic, conversion rate, cost per lead
Overlay: KNOW WHAT'S WORKING

Expected Form Completion: 20-28%

Concept 6: The Results Testimonial

Format: Vertical Video (20-25 sec)

Before: pretty site, no enquiries, unresponsive freelancers. With i3MEDIA: GreenGate 5x revenue in 18 months; Eden +20% enquiries
Overlay: SWITCH NOW

Expected CTR: 3.8-4.6%

3. Campaign and Ad Set Structure

Layer Audience Logic Creative Mix Primary Objective
Warm Intent Retargeting High-intent page viewers and form starters Concepts 1, 2 (Supplier Sprawl, Website Speed) Booked discovery-call efficiency
Mid-Funnel Re-engagement Content engagers and repeat site visitors Concepts 3, 4, 5 (AI Search, Customer Experience, Growth Audit) Qualified return traffic and lead intent
Reactivation Longer-window visitors with no conversion Concepts 5, 6 (Growth Audit, Results Testimonial) Recover stalled opportunities

4. Event and Signal Framework

  • Core events: form start, form submit, booked discovery call, and qualified meeting attended.
  • Deduplication standard: use consistent event IDs across browser and server streams.
  • Source taxonomy: retain campaign, ad set, and creative identifiers through CRM sync for downstream pipeline attribution.
  • Suppression signal: once a discovery call is booked, remove contacts from active retargeting within one sync cycle.

5. Creative Rotation and Fatigue Controls

Rotation Rules

  • Maintain at least three concept families live at the same time.
  • Refresh weakest variants every 10-14 days once stable delivery is established.
  • Keep one challenger creative per concept family active continuously.

Fatigue Rules

  • Watch rising frequency with falling qualified click-through trend.
  • Replace repetitive hooks before delivery narrows to one message family.
  • Preserve format diversity across image, video, and carousel placements.

6. Performance Review Cadence

  • Twice weekly: check CAPI health, event match quality trends, and audience suppression lag.
  • Weekly: evaluate booked discovery-call CPA and meeting quality by audience and concept family.
  • Monthly: rebalance budget toward top-performing audience windows and retire chronically weak concept families.
