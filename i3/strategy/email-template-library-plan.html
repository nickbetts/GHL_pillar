Product decision

The first release is an authenticated writing workbench inside i3 Sales. A salesperson can select a queue lead or enter details manually, choose a reviewed email angle, edit the result and copy it. It does not send email, write to GHL or persist message content.

Why deterministic copy: every claim, angle and regulated-sector caution remains reviewable. Sub-sector insights and service modules combine predictably, while salesperson edits keep the final message human.

In scope

Lead personalisation, six variants, editable subject/body, unresolved-variable warnings, copy controls and rationale.

Out of scope

Direct sending, automated sequences, open tracking, GHL writes, AI generation and prospect-specific invented audits.

Content model

18 canonical sub-sectors × 6 reviewed variants. Research and cautions never enter the copied email.

Copy principles

Expected, not automated

Requested-email copy opens naturally after a conversation. Gentle follow-up assumes the first note may simply have been missed.

One useful observation

Lead with a supportable sector pattern, then suggest a practical starting point. Never pretend a site audit happened when it did not.

Credible agency story

Explain that i3MEDIA brings strategy, design, build and marketing together, backed by more than 20 years of delivery.

Plain language

Prefer trust, clarity, discoverability and enquiry quality over jargon, hype or generic “digital transformation” claims.

Evidence before claims

Any performance, accreditation, outcome or superiority statement must be supplied and verified for the specific prospect or case study.

Regulated-sector restraint

No guarantees, cure claims, fear tactics, implied endorsement, legal advice or unsupported claims of safety and success.

Delivery workflow

01 · Enter

Open from the queue

The envelope action deep-links with the lead ID. The page loads current authenticated queue data.

02 · Confirm

Check the audience

Name, company and sub-sector are filled where available. The salesperson corrects any missing or ambiguous data.

03 · Choose

Select the email job

Use requested follow-up, gentle chase, website, SEO, paid media or joined-up growth.

04 · Review

Edit the draft

The subject and body stay editable. Internal rationale explains the angle and highlights the claims boundary.

05 · Resolve

Complete variables

Warnings identify missing personalisation. Copying unresolved text requires explicit confirmation.

06 · Send

Copy to email

The salesperson copies the reviewed draft into their normal email workflow and owns the final send.

Quality assurance

Matrix completeness

Automated tests require 18 sub-sectors, six variants and all 108 generated combinations.

Token handling

Known variables resolve as plain editable text. Unknown or missing values remain visible.

Claims guardrails

Every profile carries a caution, and generated copy is checked for prohibited guarantee language.

Lead deep links

Valid IDs select the current queue record; stale IDs fall back to manual fields without blocking work.

Responsive use

The two-column workbench becomes a stacked editor on mobile without hiding controls or copy actions.

Security boundary

Queue data remains behind existing session auth. The page adds no API mutation or database path.
