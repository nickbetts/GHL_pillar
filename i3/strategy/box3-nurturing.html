i3MEDIA GTM Playbook: Nurture and Outbound

Relationship-layer execution for automated email drips and personal outbound through LinkedIn.

Nurturing | Newsletters & Automated Email Drips

Relationship Layer

1. The 2026 Digital Landscape Context Hooks

Every automated follow-up sequence should tie into a current, relevant shift so the content feels timely and useful:

  • AI search shift angle: Position i3MEDIA as helping brands stay discoverable as buyers move to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI answers. Keep claims specific and evidence-led.
  • Marketing consent angle: Emphasise clean, compliant email and consent practices (PECR/GDPR) as part of a joined-up marketing setup, validating any legal interpretation with up-to-date ICO guidance before publication.

2. Low-Friction Lead Magnets

Capture contact entries through useful resources before pitching a full discovery call:

  • Resource Asset A: How to Choose a Digital Agency: The 10-Point Checklist for Getting It Right.
  • Resource Asset B: The 2026 AI Search Playbook: Getting Your Brand Found in ChatGPT and Google AI Answers.

3. Step-by-Step Value-First Email Drip Workflows

Email 1: Immediate Asset Delivery & Quick Win

  • Timing: Sent instantly upon download trigger.
  • Subject: Your Guide to Choosing a Digital Agency [Attached]
  • Body Delivery Profile: Deliver the PDF link. Add a warm practical note: pick one quick win from the guide (for example, fixing your homepage headline or page speed) and show briefly how i3MEDIA helps teams action these without the usual back-and-forth.

Email 2: The Core Pain Point (The Patchwork Problem)

  • Timing: 3 days post Email 1.
  • Subject: How much momentum are your marketing suppliers costing you?
  • Body Delivery Profile: Plain text, peer-to-peer execution style. Address the hidden cost of coordinating a designer, an SEO and a paid-ads freelancer who never quite align. Focus directly on lost time, mixed messaging, and results left on the table.

Email 3: The Leadership Validation Layer (Proving ROI)

  • Timing: 4 days post Email 2.
  • Subject: The 3 questions your board will ask about marketing next quarter
  • Body Delivery Profile: Target the strategic questions leadership asks: which channels drive enquiries, what is our cost per lead, and are we visible in AI search. Explain how fragmented suppliers obscure these answers, and provide a clean one-page reporting layout.

Email 4: Proof-Led Walkthrough Invitation

  • Timing: 7 days post Email 3.
  • Subject: How Eden Shopping Centre lifted retailer enquiries by 20%
  • Body Delivery Profile: Present a clear case study showing a real-world transformation in results. Conclude with a direct call-to-action to book a discovery call.

Nurturing | LinkedIn Personal Outbound Pipeline

One-to-One Personal Prospecting

1. Platform Safety Constraints & Algorithmic Boundaries

Outbound prospecting from the Founder's personal page must follow strict platform rules to protect the profile from account restrictions:

  • Strict Volume Ceilings: Limit connection invites to a strict maximum of **20–30 requests per day** (capped at 100–120 per week).
  • Acceptance Rate Classifiers: Maintain a non-sales, value-first delivery style to ensure your invite acceptance metrics stay between **30%–45%**. Falling below a 20% baseline acceptance threshold can flag the profile as automated spam.
  • Infrastructure Guardrails: Do not deploy local browser Chrome extensions that share local IP footprints. Use native cloud-based prospecting platforms backed by dedicated residential proxies.

2. The 30-Minute Daily Operational Block Template

Implement this exact structured sequence to make outbound loops efficient and consistent:

Time Block Allocation Target Operational Action Execution Parameter Guide
Minutes 1–10 Substantive Content Engagement Leave 3–5 in-depth comments on targeted marketing and business-leader profiles. Avoid short generic text.
Minutes 11–20 Personalised Connection Invites Send 5–10 requests containing tailored notes based on the prospect's role.
Minutes 21–30 Conversational Follow-up Loops Engage with accepted connections, manage your inbox, and sync data back to i3MEDIA tracking tables.
