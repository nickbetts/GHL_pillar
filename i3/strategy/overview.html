Brand Positioning

One-line position: i3MEDIA is the UK full-service digital agency that designs, builds and markets under one roof — one integrated team, from strategy to execution, making technology work harder for your business.

What that means in practice

i3MEDIA operates as one joined-up delivery team across brand, website, SEO, paid media, and lifecycle channels. That means fewer delays between strategy and execution, no hand-offs between separate agencies, and campaigns that can be scaled quickly once they prove impact.

For 20+ years we have delivered for ambitious organisations across retail & eCommerce, charity, healthcare, education, and industry — from Eden Shopping Centre to Bombay Deli to national charities. We keep the same operating logic for every account: diagnose, build, launch, measure, and optimise.

Brand Voice

We sound like

  • Warm and human. We talk to marketing and leadership teams like people, not procurement committees.
  • Practical and direct. No fluff, no hassle. Real outcomes, plain language.
  • Confident and outcome-led. We lead with the result the client gets, not our internal process.
  • Quietly confident. We know what we do well. We do not need to shout it.
  • British. Organised, understated, and occasionally self-aware.

We do not sound like

  • Corporate jargon that sounds like a consultancy deck
  • Preachy or moralistic copy
  • Overly casual internet slang
  • Cold, technical wording with no human tone
  • Vague filler with no clear meaning

Tone by Channel

ChannelToneExample
Google Ads headlines Direct and outcome-focused. One clear idea per line. "One agency, from strategy to growth" not "Complex multi-service message"
Meta ad copy Conversational opener, specific pain, warm payoff. "If your website looks great but isn't bringing in enquiries, this is for you."
LinkedIn company posts Useful and grounded. Teach something small, then let it land. "Most teams do not lose growth because they lack ideas. They lose it in the gaps between strategy, design, content, and paid media."
Founder LinkedIn posts First-person, direct, occasionally dry. Real opinions, not corporate safe. "A client came to us with a beautiful website that Google had never heard of. Design without discoverability is just decoration."
Email subject lines Plain and curious. Reads like it came from a real person. "Is your website actually bringing in enquiries?" not "Your Q2 Digital Growth Resource Is Ready"
Landing page headlines Reassuring and clear. Tell them what it is and why it matters. "Design, build and marketing under one roof" not "Generic infrastructure wording"

Things we always say

  • Full-service and under one roof. Strategy, design, build and marketing sit in one integrated team
  • Measurable outcomes over vanity metrics: enquiries, qualified pipeline, conversion quality, and revenue
  • Cross-sector capability: retail & eCommerce, charity, healthcare, education, and industry
  • Built for how people search now: technical SEO plus AI & answer-engine optimisation (AEO/GEO)
  • Evidence-led decision making: attribution clarity, channel accountability, and weekly keep/kill reviews
  • 20+ years, hundreds of projects, and relationships that run for years, not months

Messaging Pillars

Every page should ladder up to these four pillars.

  • One team, end to end. Strategy, design, build and marketing under one roof — no hand-offs between separate agencies.
  • 20 years, real results. Hundreds of projects and long-running retainer relationships across every sector (Eden, Bombay Deli, national charities).
  • Built for how people search now. Technical SEO plus AI & answer-engine optimisation (AIO/AEO/GEO) — the space most agencies have not caught up on yet.
  • Outcomes, not vanity. We report on enquiries, pipeline, conversions and revenue. We make technology work harder for your business.

Category Positioning Route

Primary message: i3MEDIA is the UK full-service digital agency for ambitious organisations. Secondary proof route: a visible cross-sector portfolio — retail & eCommerce, charity, healthcare and education — backed by 20+ years of delivery.

  • Category framing: full-service digital design, build and marketing under one roof
  • Proof framing: 20+ years, in-house delivery model, and a visible portfolio across sectors
  • Standard CTA: "Book a Discovery Call" everywhere; secondary CTA "See our work"
  • Execution rule: any superlative claim must have a dated source note and evidence table in the Trust Sprint page
