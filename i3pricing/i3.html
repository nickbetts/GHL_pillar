1. Commercial Comparison Matrix

Operational Layer Our Approach New Standards
1. Analytics & Signals Browser-only tracking that is often blocked, leaving gaps in lead and sales reporting. Server-side tracking through the client domain for more reliable conversion reporting.
2. Bidding Efficiency Campaigns optimised only for top-line revenue. Campaigns optimised for profit signals so spend favours better-margin outcomes.
3. Conversion Loops Slow form-first journeys that lose mobile users before contact is made. Faster enquiry routes with clear calls to action and cleaner mobile handover.
4. Data Compliance Manual list uploads with weak governance and unclear handling standards. Structured first-party data processes with privacy controls and clear audit trail.
5. Creative Performance One-size-fits-all ad creative with very little variation. Purpose-built creative sets tested by channel, audience, and offer angle.
6. Web Speed Performance Heavy website builds that load slowly on mobile devices. Lean front-end delivery and speed-first pages designed to convert paid traffic.
7. Portals & Upgrades Single-stack systems that are hard to update and easy to bottleneck. Decoupled delivery with cleaner pathways for updates, dashboards, and client portals.
8. Human Form Checks Intrusive form checks that add friction and reduce completions. Low-friction form protection that preserves user experience.
9. Paid Traffic Defense Fraud checks only after spend has already been lost. Edge-level controls that reduce invalid traffic before it wastes budget.
10. Search and Social Structure Fragmented campaign structures with poor data flow. Unified campaign structure with clear testing, budgeting, and reporting standards.
11. Authority Building Low-quality outreach and content with weak relevance. Topical outreach and expert-led content that supports long-term rankings.
12. Consent and Privacy Cookie and consent tools that are poorly configured and hard to maintain. Consent and privacy controls configured properly with lightweight implementation.

3. Commercial Terms and Delivery Rules

  • Retainer term: Core Marketing Setup and Lead-Gen Fast-Track have a 3-month minimum, then move to rolling monthly. Active Acquisition Suite and Total Growth Solution are sold on 6-month or 12-month contracts.
  • Rolling cancellation: 30 days written notice.
  • Billing: Retainers are billed monthly in advance. The ad spend management fee is billed in arrears from actual platform invoices. Delivery may pause if invoices remain overdue.
  • Ad spend: Always paid directly by the client to platforms.
  • Scaling fee: Per client, 10% applies to ad spend above £5,000 up to £20,000, then 7.5% applies to the portion above £20,000.
  • Prepay discount: Discounted prepay is available on 12-month agreements only.
  • Annual uplift: Annual pricing updates follow CPI only.
  • Minimum fee floor: Minimum monthly fee floor is set at quote stage.
  • Overage billing: Out-of-scope overage is billed in 1-hour increments.
  • Ownership: Client owns account access, account history, and performance data. i3MEDIA-hosted landing pages, Performance Sites, and Headless Shopify ecommerce builds are not transferred on exit. In-house WordPress websites are transferable.
  • Scope control: Any work outside listed deliverables moves to bolt-on pricing or a separate scoped quote.
