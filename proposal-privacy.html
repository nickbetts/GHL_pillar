Proposal and e-signature privacy notice

Last reviewed 15 October 2026

This short notice explains what i3MEDIA Ltd records when you open a proposal or contract on your private link and sign it electronically. It supplements the main i3MEDIA privacy policy, which remains the full policy.

What we record

When you open and sign a document we record your name, the email address you used to open it, your signature, the date and time, and your IP address. We also record details of the device you used: its browser and operating system, language, time zone, screen size and similar technical settings. We combine these into a device ID, which helps show that the person who signed was you.

Why we record it

To confirm who signed, to keep reliable evidence that you agreed to the contract, and to help prevent fraud and disputes. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in keeping that evidence, and the performance of the contract itself. We do not use these details for marketing, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell them.

Where it goes

The details are stored with your signed contract in our proposal system, and the main ones are shown on the signed contract you can see. They are available to the i3MEDIA team working on your project. We use appropriate service providers to host and operate the system.

How long we keep it

We keep it with the signed contract for as long as we need it as evidence of the agreement, normally the length of the contract plus six years.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used, although we may need to keep signing records where the law lets us or where we need them to deal with a legal claim. Contact data@i3media.net with "DATA PRIVACY REQUEST" in the subject line. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
