01 / The Stream

One continuous flow, from first dial to signed deal.

Every step hands off to the next, so nothing gets dropped between tools.

  1. 01
    Source

    Load a prioritised queue

    Import from Apollo, Google Maps or CSV and stack the best-fit sectors to the top. Duplicates are caught at the door.

    Learn more
  2. 02
    Call

    Talk to the right people

    Zen-mode calling puts one lead on screen with click-to-call, notes and outcomes. The next lead loads on its own.

    Learn more
  3. 03
    Qualify

    Score and route every enquiry

    Forms, landing pages and webhooks land in one inbox, scored, tagged and sent to the right rep in seconds.

    Learn more
  4. 04
    Propose

    Send proposals clients can sign

    Branded proposals, a private client link and e-signature with a full audit trail, then an onboarding questionnaire.

    Learn more
