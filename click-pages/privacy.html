Privacy notice

Financial adviser campaign page · Last reviewed 14 August 2026

This short notice explains how i3MEDIA handles information submitted through this campaign page. It supplements the main i3MEDIA privacy policy, which remains the full policy.

What we collect

We collect the details you choose to submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, firm name and message. We also record campaign information such as the landing page, referring page, UTM values and advertising click identifiers where they are present.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, arrange a call, assign the enquiry to the relevant i3MEDIA representative and understand which campaign generated it. We do not sell your details.

Where it goes

Submissions are stored in i3MEDIA's internal sales system and may be accessible to the assigned i3MEDIA team member. We use appropriate service providers to host and operate the system.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its use for marketing. Contact data@i3media.net with “DATA PRIVACY REQUEST” in the subject line. You can also contact the ICO.
